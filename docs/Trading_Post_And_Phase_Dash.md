# Trading Post and Phase Dash

Canguit: Trading Post replaces Merchant Steps, costs 120 gold, and applies only to new training orders. Up to two living upgraded units reserve separate path positions next to the deployment node closest to the beginning of the route. They walk there, stop, and keep earning their normal income. They remain members of the enemy-target groups: towers can shoot them and guards can intercept them. They gain no health, invulnerability, or range immunity. Additional units remain mobile; destroyed posts free a slot for newly spawned units. A stationary post does not occupy the defender deployment node.

Ronel: Phase Dash replaces Warded Cloak, costs 120 gold, and applies only to new training orders. Being blocked automatically activates 2.5 seconds of guard immunity with 40% extra speed. The 12-second cooldown starts upon activation. Guard pursuit and contact acquisition both respect the immunity; once it expires Ronel can be blocked again. Tower attacks, burn and other damage still apply throughout. This is an escape skill, not a damage skill.

Both lists retain five choices and the two-purchase limit. New 2D SVG icons appear in the existing upgrade sidebar. Above-unit status labels and the selected-troop panel display active abilities. Tune duration, cooldown, speed multiplier, and stationary cap in resources/attacker/combat_balance.tres; prices are in the individual upgrade Resources.
